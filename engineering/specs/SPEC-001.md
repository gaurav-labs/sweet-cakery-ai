# SPEC-001

## Functional Requirements

### Product Management

**FR-001** AI can create a new WooCommerce product.

**FR-002** AI can update an existing product.

**FR-003** AI can generate product title, descriptions and SEO content.

**FR-004** AI can upload product images.

**FR-005** AI can create product variations using predefined business rules.

**FR-006** AI must ask for approval before publishing a product.

---

### Customer Experience

**FR-007** AI can chat with customers.

**FR-008** AI can answer questions about products, flavours and ordering.

**FR-009** AI can recommend suitable cakes.

**FR-010** AI can help customers customise cakes.

**FR-011** AI can prepare a shopping cart.

**FR-012** AI can check order status.

**FR-013** AI can generate a WhatsApp link containing the customer's order details.

---

### Platform

**FR-014** AI can use external tools.

**FR-015** AI can remember the current conversation.

**FR-016** AI can access approved business knowledge.

**FR-017** Every important AI action must be reviewable.

**FR-018** Every important AI action must be traceable.

**FR-019** Business rules remain under user control.
